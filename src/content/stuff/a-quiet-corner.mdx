## The idea

A website does not need to explain everything about its maker. Sometimes a
name, a few things made with care, and a place to put them are enough.

This experiment imagines a quiet archive: soft violet, a translucent surface,
and enough space to let each small piece breathe.

## The shape of it

- A home for a pseudonym, without a résumé or personal timeline.
- A shelf for work, including the unfinished kind.
- A notebook for observations that might be useful later.

## What I learned

Making something smaller is often harder than adding another section. The
useful question became: *does this help someone understand the work?*

> Leave enough room for the next idea.

## Next, perhaps

Better keyboard navigation, a few more sketches, and fewer reasons to wait
until everything feels finished.
