I originally just wanted a name that would fit a maid character. Somehow, I ended up with VelvetMaid.

## Velvet

When I was a kid, there was this fabric I really liked playing with. I don't remember what it was called, but I loved running my fingers across its surface.

Whenever I brushed it in the opposite direction, the color seemed to change. I could draw things on it with just my fingers, then brush them away and start over.

I'm still not entirely sure what that fabric was. It might have been velvet, or maybe corduroy. Apparently, corduroy is sometimes called "poor man's velvet."

But hey, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YjYrfV2xIwI&list=OLAK5uy_knj6lw9T3B9va1hkHQhXAaCy0PmodZMf8&index=10" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Velvet Underground</a> is pretty cool too, right?

## Maid

This one's much simpler.

I've always liked maid outfits in anime, especially the character designs.

There's no deeper meaning behind it. I just think they look cool.

That's pretty much it.
